Privacy Policy — Essayer

Last updated: November 5, 2026 · Français

Essayer offers one small challenge a day. The app has no server, creates no account and collects no personal data. Your tries stay on your device; only anonymous statistics are shared, as explained below.

Your tries

When you tap "I tried", the app saves the date and the challenge of the day only on your iPhone, to grow your tree and show your history. This data is never sent to us and is deleted along with the app.

Anonymous statistics (iCloud)

To learn which challenges people like and to show how many people tried today's challenge, Essayer sends, when you tap "I tried" or "Not for me", an anonymous record containing only the date, the challenge text and, if any, the reason you chose. These records are stored in Apple's public iCloud database (CloudKit). They contain neither your name, nor your email address, nor your Apple ID, and are never used for advertising or tracking. If you are not signed in to iCloud, or if you turn off sharing in the app's Settings, nothing is sent and the app works normally.

Notifications

If you allow them, Essayer schedules a reminder each morning with the challenge of the day, and a follow-up in the late afternoon if you haven't tried it yet. These are local notifications, prepared by the app on your device: no information goes through a server. You can choose their times or turn them off in the app's Settings, or in Settings → Notifications → Essayer.

What we don't do

No accounts, no advertising, no trackers, no third-party analytics, no selling of data. Essayer contains no third-party tracking SDK and does not access your contacts, your photos or your location.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data about children.

Changes

This policy may be updated; the date at the top of the page shows the latest revision.
