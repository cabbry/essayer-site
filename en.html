One challenge a day

Write a note to someone, call a friend you've lost touch with, say thank you… Hundreds of small challenges, plus seasonal ones.

A growing cherry tree

Every try makes the tree and its vines grow. It starts over every season and reaches full bloom after 30 tries.

The four seasons

Cherry blossoms in spring, green leaves in summer, red maples in autumn, snow in winter: the garden follows the calendar.

No pressure

Nothing is lost if you miss a day. A gentle reminder in the morning, a follow-up in the late afternoon if needed, and that's it.

Together

Frequently asked questions

  • Not getting notifications? Check that notifications are allowed in Settings → Notifications → Essayer, then open the app once to reschedule the reminders.
  • Change the reminder times? In the app, tap the gear icon: you can turn the morning reminder and the late-afternoon follow-up on or off, and choose the time of each.
  • My tree got small again? That's normal: it starts over at the beginning of each season (March 1, June 1, September 1, December 1). All your tries stay in the history.
  • Can I complete several challenges a day? No, just one: today's. A new challenge awaits you tomorrow.
